Same GPU and usage window. Both examples include the $22 upfront payment and exclude taxes or other charges.

Rental price rises to $4 an hour

Use the price you booked.

Renting separately would cost $400. Use your secured price and pay $300, plus $20 protection and $2 booking: $322 total, or $78 less.

Rental price falls to $2 an hour

Rent elsewhere for less.

If comparable compute is available for $200, you can rent it elsewhere. With the $22 already paid, that’s $222 total. Using your booked price would cost $322.

A cheaper rental may not be available. Selling your protection would change these totals.

About Dodona

What we’re building

Dodona is building a way to book GPU rental prices ahead of time. Suppliers would back the compute; Dodona would arrange bookings, resales, and buybacks.

We’re still working on supplier agreements, pricing, and access terms. This demo doesn’t take payments or provide compute.

Questions

Before you sign up

Can I buy protection now?+

No. This is a demo. The waitlist is for updates and possible early access.

Is this insurance?+

It serves a similar purpose: protect your budget from rising costs. It’s a compute pricing agreement, not an insurance policy. You’d get the choice to rent the agreed compute at a fixed price, rather than an expense reimbursement.

How would I get the compute?+

Request access by the agreed deadline and pay the booked rental price. The provider would give you GPU access for the agreed window. You’re renting compute, not buying hardware.

Who backs the compute?+

The plan is to use reserved capacity or binding provider commitments. Supplier agreements, capacity checks, and terms for failed delivery are still being developed.

Can I sell it for what I paid?+

You could ask that price, but a buyer has to agree. A resale fee applies when the sale completes. The buyer gets your remaining compute access; your booking fee stays paid.

How would a buyback work?+

A supplier would commit funds to buy the protection. Dodona would show you a payout based on its current value, less processing and a service margin. You could accept or try selling to another user. A quote isn’t guaranteed.

What fees would I pay?+

Protection and booking are paid upfront. Compute is paid for if you use it. Selling to another user adds a transaction fee; a buyback includes the deduction shown in its quote. You’d see the fees before confirming.

Is the booking fee refundable?+

The plan is to keep the booking fee whether you use, sell, accept a buyback, or let the protection expire, subject to required refund rights. Supplier failures and refunds of the protection payment need separate terms; those aren’t final yet.

What happens to my email?+

We use it for product updates and possible early access. We keep signup details until you ask us to remove them. Read our signup policy or email us to unsubscribe.
