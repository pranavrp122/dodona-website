What matters most to Picus, in order

  1. A working two-sided product on real data. Seed investors buy execution. Say how fast this was built and that the pricing engine runs on the live index.
  2. The gap and the timing. No compute futures trade yet: CME's contract is in CFTC review until 9 Nov 2026 and ICE has no date. Gammon Capital wrote on 12 Oct 2026 that reserved capacity is the only deep instrument and that caps and options are bilateral and bespoke. The mega-deals prove the need: Nvidia's $36B of capacity commitments, Meta's up to $15B with Nebius, CoreWeave's $6.3B backstop. Nothing exists for the long tail.
  3. Why neoclouds say yes. Idle hours are real: Azure production traces show peak load 3.3x the valley for conversation and 34.6x for coding, with weekends much lower. Covered calls on those hours are premium income with no new liability beyond hours they already own. Floors lower their cost of debt.
  4. The analogy they already own. terralayr sells battery capacity from 15 minutes to 15 years to customers including data centres, with tolling deals. Axle plugs fragmented flexible assets into wholesale markets. Quantile is a power-trading desk Picus led. Dodona is terralayr's contract model applied to GPU hours, with the option layer on top.
  5. Fees that hold up. Deribit charges 3 basis points of notional capped at 12.5% of premium; OKX caps at 7% of premium. Dodona's 15% is richer because the product bundles a physical capacity hold and delivery, not just a price.
  6. Risks you name first. The Ornn index is demonstration-only and needs a licence. Counsel must opine on a pre-expiry order book. Delivery risk sits with the neocloud and is managed with capacity holds, KYB and the escrow agent. Liquidity starts on the supply side with neocloud-posted offers.

Why options are the product operators need

  • A buyer wants a ceiling with an exit, the way they buy insurance. A future removes the upside and adds margin calls.
  • Non-financial companies do not run margin accounts. A CFO can budget a fee.
  • Capacity is the real risk. A future cannot deliver GPUs; a Dodona cap is tied to a reservation.
  • The supply side has idle hours and expensive debt. Covered calls monetise the hours; floors protect the debt.
  • Futures and options are complementary. Futures give the index price discovery; options are what operators buy. When CME or ICE list, Dodona hedges its book on them. Ornn is building the benchmark; Dodona builds the product that uses it, and needs Ornn's licence to do it.
  • Precedents: EEX power options, Irish capacity options, tolling agreements, Southwest's fuel hedges, producer collars.
  • Volatility makes it worth paying for. Silicon Data reports 26.2% annualised for H100 and 30.2% for B200; the Ornn-based figure is 38 to 89% depending on sampling. The deck's example: 64 H100 for a week, a $2.50 cap for a $0.25 fee.

Objections and short answers

Isn't this a reserved instance?Reserved is a lock with no exit. A cap is an exit plus a ceiling. Reserved discounts run 17 to 33%, up to 60% on long terms; a cap often costs less than that discount and keeps the flexibility.
Why won't Ornn or a big neocloud do this?Ornn is an index and futures business. A neocloud selling its own caps has a conflict and no cross-provider liquidity. Dodona is neutral and never takes the other side.
Where does liquidity come from?Supply first: neoclouds post offers and sell covered calls on idle hours. Demand from AI labs and model companies. Resale of delivered hours is the secondary market from day one; a pre-expiry book comes after counsel's opinion.
Regulatory?Physical trade options between commercial parties need no exchange licence. Any cash book-out has to be a separate later agreement, which is why the product resells hours instead. Counsel confirms the order book. No offshore structure.
Data?Ornn is demonstration-only until licensed. Silicon Data is the alternative. Over time, executed contracts produce Dodona's own prints.
Delivery and credit risk?KYB on both sides, capacity holds confirmed by the neocloud, delivery-backed tiers, an escrow agent, and Dodona never holds funds.
