How the chain works

Each entry's hash is SHA-256 of the previous entry's hash, a newline, and the entry's fields (id, time, kind, ref, summary, data) as JSON with sorted keys and no spaces. The first entry links to 64 zeros. Changing any past entry changes its hash and breaks every link after it. The verify button fetches the latest entries and recomputes every hash in your browser, without trusting the server's own check.
